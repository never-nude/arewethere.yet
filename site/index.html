How to read the evidence and the years

Each marker stands for one condition the Watch requires. Independent findings for the same system or its successors can confirm or rule out a condition. Once every condition has a finding, the evidence score is the share met, expressed out of 100. An unresolved condition keeps the total open.

Superintelligence includes the abilities required for AGI. A specialist system could improve its successor before it reaches AGI. For a singularity, that feedback would also have to speed up technological progress more broadly.
