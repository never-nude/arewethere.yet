What the terms mean

General intelligence describes an ability. The other four terms name the milestones this Watch tracks.

General intelligence
The ability to learn across different kinds of problems and apply that learning in an unfamiliar situation. People and other animals can show it.
Artificial general intelligence (AGI)
An AI that reliably performs at the level of skilled people across many kinds of unfamiliar work.
Recursive self-improvement (RSI)
An AI helps build a better successor. That successor then uses its improved abilities to help build a better one in turn.
Artificial superintelligence (ASI)
An AI that reliably and substantially outperforms leading human experts across many intellectual fields.
The singularity
AI systems help build better successors, and that cycle keeps speeding up technological progress beyond the lab.
