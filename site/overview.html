How the four milestones fit together

General intelligence (AGI) would match skilled people across many fields. Superintelligence (ASI) would surpass leading experts. Recursive self-improvement (RSI) could begin with a research specialist that helps build a stronger successor. A singularity would mean each new generation makes that improvement faster.
