Milestone field

What the terms mean

General intelligence describes an ability. The other four terms name the milestones this Watch tracks.

General intelligence
The ability to learn across different kinds of problems and apply that learning in an unfamiliar situation.

A person who learns to plan a household budget can use the same reasoning to organize a community event. Researchers study this ability in people and other animals. The term itself sets no particular level of skill.

Artificial general intelligence (AGI)
An AI that reliably performs at the level of skilled people across many kinds of unfamiliar work.

Independent evaluators would give the same system fresh problems in areas such as reasoning, language and practical planning. It would need to apply what it learned to new tasks and repeat its performance.

Recursive self-improvement (RSI)
An AI helps build a better successor. That successor then uses its improved abilities to help build a better one in turn.

Suppose one system discovers a better training method. Its successor would have to use its improved capabilities to produce a further gain. A controlled comparison would trace each gain to the system that contributed it.

Artificial superintelligence (ASI)
An AI that reliably and substantially outperforms leading human experts across many intellectual fields.

Beating a chess champion establishes strength in chess. The Watch would look for the same AI to outperform leading experts on fresh problems across many fields, with independent teams reproducing the results.

The singularity
AI systems help build better successors, and that cycle keeps speeding up technological progress beyond the lab.

The Watch would look for AI-led research to make successive generations improve faster, then for that acceleration to spread beyond one lab. Independent teams would need to separate the effect of AI from other changes, such as adding more computing power.

Under these definitions, artificial superintelligence includes artificial general intelligence. A specialized system could begin recursive self-improvement earlier. The singularity requires separate evidence that the pace of progress has changed more widely. Explore these connections.
