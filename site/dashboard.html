    Which milestones can come first?

    ASI would also meet the AGI bar. Under the Watch’s definitions, AGI performs at least as well as skilled people across many kinds of unfamiliar work. ASI consistently beats leading experts across that same range. The first evidence for AGI could come earlier or in the same evaluation. [6]

    Recursive self-improvement can start at different times. Even a specialist could help build a stronger successor that then helps improve the next model. That loop could start before AGI, between AGI and ASI, or after ASI. [9]

    Compare three possible orders
    • AGI → ASI → RSI. Engineers build broadly superhuman AI, then use it to help develop stronger successors.
    • AGI → RSI → ASI. A broadly capable AI starts helping to build successors while people still outperform it on expert tasks. Later improvements could clear the higher bar.
    • RSI → AGI → ASI. A research specialist starts a feedback loop first. Later systems could become broadly capable if those gains carry over to other kinds of work.

    The Singularity would require the loop to keep speeding up. It could level off if resources or testing become bottlenecks. The three evidence checks concern reliable autonomy, repeated research, and accelerating gains attributable to AI. The Watch can assess them independently of the AGI and ASI labels.

          How this works

          Evidence, scenarios, and labels

          The index totals weighted evidence points. We choose the weights and reference targets. Missing readings leave their points unfilled and lower source coverage.

          Two indicators can rise together for several reasons. For example, a higher benchmark score and a shorter research cycle could both reflect more computing power. To confirm a singularity, independent researchers would need to show that AI’s contribution caused successive generations to improve faster.

          AGI and ASI both require broad skills on this Watch. ASI sets a higher performance bar. Recursive self-improvement describes how one AI generation helps improve the next. See which milestones can come first ↑

          The automated readings can contribute up to 50 of the 100 points for AGI and 40 for ASI. The remaining points require an independent evaluation and editorial review. A scheduled source check can carry forward an older finding.

            How sources are selected

            We check the author, publisher, editorial oversight, and relevance to the claim. We use peer-reviewed research and evaluations from qualified institutions for technical evidence. Primary records provide their own dated measurements, statements, or historical accounts.

            For interpretation, we prefer bylined independent reporting that identifies its evidence and distinguishes reporting from opinion. Interviews and viewpoints are labeled. We apply the same standards across editorial perspectives. Wikipedia’s reliable-source guidance ↗ · Perennial source guidance ↗

            We exclude the New York Post, Newsmax, bot accounts, AI-generated articles, and unattributed advocacy. The benchmark data, journal papers, and reporting link to their original sources.

            “Stay ready so you don’t have to get ready.”

            When a headline sounds alarming, check what was measured and where the finding applies. Look for independent follow-up. At work or in your community, ask who reviews AI-assisted decisions and what would trigger a change in practice.
